### **Student Activity: Exploring Amazon's DevOps Journey**

---

Welcome to the hands-on session where you'll explore Amazon's DevOps journey through practical activities. This session is designed for beginners and will guide you through key concepts and practices that Amazon implemented to transform its infrastructure. Follow the steps and practice each activity on your own.

---

### **1. Understanding DevOps**

**Activity 1: Define DevOps**

- **Task:** Write a brief definition of DevOps in your own words.
- **Objective:** Understand the core idea of DevOps as a practice that integrates development and operations.

**Activity 2: Identify DevOps Tools**

- **Task:** Research and list three popular DevOps tools used for automation and integration.
- **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with tools that facilitate DevOps practices.

**Activity 3: DevOps Benefits**

- **Task:** List three benefits of adopting DevOps in software development.
- **Objective:** Recognize the advantages of implementing DevOps in an organization.
